## Why it is `needs-info` again rather than `ready-for-agent`

**Half of the Android decision is not expressible.** The Apple half is: `"book"`
joins the `MEMBER_TITLE_SUBPAGES` carve-out generator, and two new exclusion
components carve out a member's book Lists. The Android member-List half is:
`/[^/]+/list/.*` becomes one pattern per private `LIST_TYPES` entry, which drops
`/book/list/x`, `/series/list/x` and `/alice/list/book/…` and follows
`LIST_TYPES` at launch on its own.

The Android **profile-tab** half is not. `/book/stats`, `/books/movies`,
`/author/activity`, `/author/reviews` and `/series/shows` are matched by
`/[^/]+/{tab}`, and `pathAdvancedPattern` is `PATTERN_ADVANCED_GLOB`: character
classes, `.`, `*`, `+`, `{n,m}`, no alternation, no lookahead. There is no "any
segment except `book`". The options are a ~300-pattern prefix trie, an
API-35-only mechanism (`uri-relative-filter-group`, or Dynamic App Links
`exclude` components in `assetlinks.json`), or leaving the five pins at
`android: true`. Dropping the tab patterns instead would unclaim
`/{member}/stats` for shows and movies.

That is a cost question, not one `CONTEXT.md` or `docs/adr/**` settles, so under
§2 it is `needs-info`, and under the project's own "a forced cut is a question to
Felipe before publishing, never a decision listed after" it had to be asked
rather than recorded. The comment asks for **one letter**, says (a) is what I
would pick, and attaches the finished brief for (a) so the next pass promotes it
with no further work.

## Calls recorded in the brief

- **Apple's two exclusions sit before the `/*/show/*` claim (line 347), not
  merely before `/*/list/*` (349).** `*` spans `/` in an AASA component, so
  `/{member}/list/book/show/comments` — a real route, slug `show` — matches
  `/*/show/*` first. Probed at `origin/main`: it is `apple: true` today.
- **They are spread conditionally on `!LIST_TYPES.has("book")`**, so adding
  `book` to that set at launch retires them and re-claims book Lists on both
  platforms in one move. Edit 3 already follows `LIST_TYPES`; edit 1 must not be
  gated, because `memberTarget` only handles the `show` and `movie` sections.
- **Criterion 4 is an allowlist, not a blacklist**: every `pathAdvancedPattern`
  must match `/^[A-Za-z0-9/_.\-*+{},[\]^]*$/`. Verified it passes over all 13
  current patterns. A `|`/`(`/`?=` blacklist misses `\d`, `?` and `^`/`$`.
- No `decisions` block: Felipe owned the product call, and the rest are
  mechanical. Nothing needed a cited rule, so nothing could be refused for one.
