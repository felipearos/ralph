## What the change does

A Pending Intent record now carries `runId` (a nonce minted once per app
process) and `capturedAt`. `consumePendingIntent` refuses an **unbound** record
(`userId === null`) unless `isFreshFromThisRun` holds — same run, at most 30
minutes old. Bound records are exempt from both checks, and a record written by
an older build (neither field) is refused when unbound, still consumed when
bound. Five files: `mobile/lib/pending-intent.ts`, four mobile test files, and
`CONTEXT.md`.
