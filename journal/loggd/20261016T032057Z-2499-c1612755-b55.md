## The spec hole, and two rejected attempts at fixing it

The sixth criterion flips the *first* "no longer redirects" check (`:93-94`). The spec has a
**second** one at `:107-108` asserting the same finished-setup behaviour from `/onboarding`, and
no criterion named it. A coder satisfying the seven criteria literally leaves a failing assertion.

`ralph-review-spec` ran twice and **rejected the first fix, correctly**, which is the part worth
recording. My first criterion said `:107-108` flips — the member stays unfinished through the end
of the test. Two further assertions the ticket never names break under that premise, and I
verified both myself rather than taking the report on trust:

- `:96` deep-links `/settings/data/import?job=…` with no `?onboarding=` parameter. The gate at
  `src/lib/supabase/middleware.ts:215-253` is exempt only through `isOnboardingImportHandoff`
  (`:28-34`), which requires that parameter, and `/settings` is not in `EXEMPT_PREFIXES`
  (`src/lib/onboarding-routes.ts:20-31`). It would be swept to `/onboarding` with `url.search = ""`
  (`:252`), so the "Import complete" block at `:96-105` would never render.
- `:115` asserts `toHaveURL("/")` after sign-out. `handleSignOut` only calls `router.refresh()`
  (`src/components/ui/UserAvatarDropdown.tsx:103-107`), which on `/onboarding` re-runs
  `requireViewer` and lands on `/sign-in?redirect=%2Fonboarding`.

The second pass rejected the replacement too, on two more points I accepted: "the previous
criterion" resolved to the seventh (the do-not-edit fence), not the fifth; and reading the DB
straight after the skip click is a race, because the skip writes through
`startTransition(submitOnboardingStepAction)` (`OnboardingFlow.tsx:47-60`) and the click resolves
first. Its third point — that "`profile` is the last step" could not be verified at `origin/main`
— I settled from #2479's own first criterion rather than dropping the sentence: "Step order is
`media → seen → profile` … `media` and `seen` cannot be skipped; `profile` can."

So the appended criterion takes the **other** design: it pins where the profile skip goes (the
fifth criterion only said "after the hand-off", which was the real ambiguity), and makes that skip
what finishes setup. `:107-108` and everything after it then stay correct as written. It appends an
eighth checkbox, still inside the threshold.

Appended rather than replaced, deliberately: #2499 is `ready-for-agent`, and the human-edits rule
bars *replacing* an existing `Acceptance criteria` on a ready ticket. An append destroys nothing
and is checkable against the ticket, which an amending comment would not be.
