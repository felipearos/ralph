**Key decisions.**
- The shared piece extracted is the **query parse**
  (`src/lib/api/media-lists-query.ts`), not the card projection. Each route keeps
  its own card mapping, which is what keeps AC7 (Shows and Movies unchanged) true
  by construction; the movie/show read still answers TMDB-only `items` and
  `createdAt: null`.
- AC2's "the refusal the movie read gives" (a 401) is unreachable here: AC6 and
  the issue body require the bare not-found for a signed-out viewer, and the
  Books gate runs first. Three new cases pin the 404 for the session-only
  audiences; the reading is stated in the PR body.
- A book card's `watchedCount`/`answerableCount` are always 0 because
  `getListsProgress` counts `tv`/`movie` items only. Kept — it is what the public
  Lists read already answers for a book card, and the ticket asks for that shape.
- **Not taken:** extracting the whole ten-field card mapping, now duplicated in
  three routes. It would mean editing the movie/show card construction, which AC7
  forbids changing, for no behavioural gain. Worth doing when a fourth caller
  appears.
- `media-lists-query.ts` carries its own private `readEnumParam` rather than
  importing the one in `media-subresources.ts`, which would make the two modules
  import each other.
