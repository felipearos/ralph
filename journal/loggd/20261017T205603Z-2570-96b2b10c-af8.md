## The decision: no split

#2570 is the child the previous tria iteration created when it closed #2566
(`20261017T204922Z-2566`). It came straight back as `oversize`, and the answer is
that the bucket's test is lexical.

`isOversize` (`frontier/next-tasks.ts:1200-1216`) is two thresholds: more than 8
acceptance-criteria checkboxes, or **three or more of four surface word
patterns** named anywhere in the body — `web|browser`, `mobile|ios|android`,
`api|endpoint|server`, `database|schema|migration`. #2570 has 6 checkboxes, so it
fired on surfaces: `web` (web sign-in, the web provider-throttle branch),
`mobile` (`mobile/lib/auth.tsx:45`) and `api` **and** `server` both
(`src/app/api/v1/...`, `~/lib/supabase/server`). Note `api` and `server` are the
same pattern, so three distinct patterns matched, not four.

Every one of those surfaces is named in order to be **ruled out**: the ticket's
whole point is that nothing but tests changes, and its criterion 5 forbids a
production diff. What it actually asks for is `route.test.ts`, a new
`src/app/forgot-password/page.test.tsx`, and one corrected comment line. Two of
its three gaps live in the same file, so a split would put them in two tickets;
and all children would inherit `## Staffing group: auth-reset`, so the split
would buy no parallelism at all. `isOversize`'s own docstring says the frontier
only decides whether to OFFER and the triager "is free to leave a ticket whole" —
being offered one that does not need splitting "costs a marker". That marker is
now on the issue, so the ticket will not recirculate while its body is unchanged.

**Generalisation for the next triager:** a test-coverage or diagnosis ticket that
*cites* paths across surfaces will almost always trip the oversize bucket, because
the test reads the whole body including the "do not go looking here" prose. Check
what the diff touches before planning a split. Do **not** delete the surface
mentions to game the heuristic — on #2570 the mobile line is load-bearing
guidance ("mobile sign-in spends nothing in the app's buckets, so do not go
looking for a sign-in that shares the bucket").
