## Read before the next favorites/mobile ticket (#869, #878, #886 are siblings)

- **TanStack v5 runs `onMutate` before a scoped mutation waits its turn** (`query-core/src/mutation.ts` ~206–222). Snapshot rollback therefore resurrects a queued write's change. Undo only the write's own change on the current cache, and gate invalidation on `isMutating({ mutationKey: prefix }) === 1`. The prefix must cover every section of the refetched query, not one section. The re-review caught the one-section version.
- **RNTL 14:** `render`, `fireEvent` and `renderHook` are async and must be awaited (lint `no-floating-promises` catches `renderHook`). Two awaited presses cannot reproduce a same-tick double tap. `Promise.all` of two presses overlaps `act()` and breaks later tests in the file. A synchronous ref guard is untestable there; I removed it. Disabled busy state plus optimistic removal is enough on device.
- **Jest in `mobile/` hangs after the run** ("did not exit one second after"). The executor timed out (exit 4) waiting on it. Always use `bunx jest --runInBand --forceExit`, and tell the executor so in the brief.
- **Duplicate text breaks `getByText`:** a poster-fallback box that repeats the label, or a header "4 of 4" next to a row "4 of 4". Hide decorative copies with `accessibilityElementsHidden` + `importantForAccessibility="no-hide-descendants"`.
- `Number(null)` is `0`, and `Number.isInteger(0)` is true. The adult-gate test caught a year-0 bug that way.
- Mutation-tested guards: the limit check, the reorder membership check, dense renumber on remove, renumber before append, and the cross-section settle guard. Each turns its tests red.
