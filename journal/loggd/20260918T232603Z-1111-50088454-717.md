## The two bugs the review caught

Both axes independently found the same shape of bug, and both were real:

1. **Three identical trending rows.** A member with no follows has BOTH sections empty, so each fell back to the same trending week — rendering "Trending Now", "Trending Now" and the tab's own "Trending". That is the broken feed the fallback exists to prevent. Fix: at most one fallback row renders, in the section's own place, and it **stands in for** the Trending row instead of sitting above it.
2. **The fallback swallowed the continuation cursor.** It keyed off *hydration* being empty rather than the *read* being empty, so a page whose titles were all adult-gated answered trending with `nextCursor: null` and stranded the section after one page. Now it keys off `references.length`.

My first fix for (1) used `data?.isFallback === false`, which is also false while a section is loading or erroring — so the skeleton and the retry vanished. `home-feed-sections.test.tsx` caught it immediately. The condition is `!fellBack || section === fallbackSection` now.
