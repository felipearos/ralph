## What shipped (in the PR)

- **`revokeCompedProCore`** in `src/server/queries/entitlement-write.ts`:
  - It locks the Account Authority row `FOR UPDATE`.
  - Refusals are `invalid_reason`, `same_account`, `not_found` and `no_active_comp`. A refusal writes nothing and records no event.
  - One UPDATE sets source `patreon`, clears the provenance, and runs `patreon_tier = CASE WHEN <backed> THEN tier ELSE 'none' END` and `is_pro = <backed> AND tier = 'pro'`.
  - The `entitlement_comp_revoked` Privilege Event commits in the same transaction. Its `previous_value` holds the full comp provenance, including the grant reason.
- **"Backed"** means `EXISTS patreon_link` with `patreon_id IS NOT NULL` and `pledge_amount_cents > 0`. That is the rotation audit's own backing rule, so a revocation never leaves a row the audit calls unbacked.
- **`revokeCompedPro`** is the Thin Transport, in the same order as the grant. There is a shared `refuseInvalidCompReason`, and `compRequestSchema` is used by both transports.
- **Docs:** CONTEXT.md (Comped Entitlement and Privilege Event) and `docs/security/privilege-events.md`.
