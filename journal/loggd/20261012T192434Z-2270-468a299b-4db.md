`828d5bb47`. Every wait was the same external cause: **Vercel took ~57 minutes
to post its required commit status**, on every PR pushed on this repo today.

- Rounds 1–3 (`ci`): `buildkite/loggd` green at 17:41Z, `Vercel` absent.
  Round 1 wrongly read it as a broken integration; Rounds 2–3 corrected that to

  showed a Preview deployment `success` at 18:16:36Z while the status API was

  auto-queue rule had already taken the PR on its own, so no `@mergifyio queue`
  was ever needed.

  now applying to queue draft #2310's head (committed 18:47Z). Nothing was
  requeued, nothing pushed.
