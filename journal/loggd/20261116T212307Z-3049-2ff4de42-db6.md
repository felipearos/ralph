# 2026-11-16 — #3049 Mobile: the Book screen's More by rows

**Outcome: merged.** PR #3539, squash-merged as `ae340a59d` at 21:21:49Z; issue closed
and labelled `testo-done`.

**What shipped.** The mobile Book screen draws rows under its genres: one "More by
<author>" per the book's first two author credits (up to 10 of that Author's other books,
the book on screen excluded, the role line as the caption), and the book's Book Series in
reading order with this book drawn in its place, marked and not pressable. Rows share the
Author and Book Series screens' query options so a heading opens a warm screen, never
fetch a next page (three provider reads beyond the detail), mount only inside `LoadedBook`
(the gate on the detail read), are not drawn when empty or not-found, and keep their
heading with a small per-row retry when they fail with no answer.

**Key decisions.**
- `BookSeriesLine` was kept: it is a fact line, existing tests assert its order, and it
  keeps the Book Series reachable when the row is not drawn.
- The P2 that mattered: the server keeps one entry per position
  (`src/lib/api/book-series-page.ts:36-46`), so another record of the same book can hold
  its slot. The row substitutes `currentBook` into the slot matching `book.series.position`.
  Found by the spec axis, independently suspected by correctness.
- Hiding a one-book first page nearly hid real Book Series: a page is cut in provider-row
  space *before* duplicate positions are dropped, so a first page can hold only this book
  while `hasMore` is true. The row is drawn whenever pages follow.
- Dropped findings: TanStack's default retry counting against the three-call budget; a
  stale multi-page cache refetching every cached page; rows reading against a
  cached-but-stale detail. All three follow from the ticket's own requirement that the
  rows share the screens' queries.
