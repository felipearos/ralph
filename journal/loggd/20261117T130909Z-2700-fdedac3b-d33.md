## What I decided and why

Felipe sent this back to triage on 2026-11-17 with "the rail is now live (#2548
closed), so the schema parts can ship as safe migrations". I checked that premise
and it does not hold yet: #2548 (the operator step) is closed, but **#2549 — the
coder rail that ships safe migrations as ordinary PRs — is still open and
`ready-for-agent`**, and loggd's `.ralph/prompt.md:36-45` still carries the
unchanged migrations HARD RAIL. So the triager readiness rule still sends a
migration to a person.

That alone would have been a thin answer, so I re-verified the whole ticket
against `main` at `70a4bd45a` through the mapper and found a **third** blocker
the October pass did not have:

1. **Migration (unchanged).** `account_deletion_job` is still four columns
   (`src/server/db/schema.ts:1977-1982`); next file would be `drizzle/0088_*`
   plus the `supabase/migrations/` mirror. And the cancel carve-out needs a
   `CREATE OR REPLACE` of `public.account_accepts_requests()`
   (`drizzle/0051_lock_account_deletions.sql:5-9`, a RESTRICTIVE policy on every
   RLS table) — a predicate change, so not "safe/additive" even once #2549 lands.
2. **Cancel vs. the fence (unchanged, but now with a cited contradiction).** A job
   row signs the member out and fences them out of everything
   (`account-access.ts:60,70`, `src/lib/supabase/server.ts:64-65`,
   `acting-member.ts:226,334`), so no cancel endpoint is reachable.
   `docs/account-data-lifecycle.md:3-4,22-24,72-77` states the opposite of this
   ticket in three places. `CONTEXT.md` and `docs/adr/` say nothing about grace
   periods, so there is no rule to cite and it is not a triager's call.
3. **NEW — password-less members.** Google sign-in exists on web
   (`src/app/sign-in/page.tsx:133-141`) and mobile (`mobile/lib/auth.tsx:185-190`),
   Apple on iOS (`:300-303`). Those accounts have no password to re-prove, and
   `src/lib/auth/credential-step-up.ts` is client-side and TOTP-only with no
   provider branch. Requiring a password would trap the members the #2581
   decision protects (`src/server/auth/request-account-deletion.ts:57-60`, App
   Store Guideline 5.1.1(v): "never a refusal, so no member is trapped").

## Why I did not split it

By the letter of the project's oversize rule it qualifies (it names web, mobile,
api and db). I deliberately left it whole: blocker 3 sits on criterion 1 and
blocker 2 on criterion 2, so **every** child of a split would still be
`ready-for-human`. Splitting would have produced four tickets no agent could take
and a spec parent for no gain. The comment names the four-child split that becomes
available the moment Felipe answers (a) how a password-less member re-proves
identity and (b) whether cancel is a fence carve-out or a narrowing of "closed".
