## What shipped

Each Author Role gets a tab with its book count on the mobile Author screen,
drawn with the shared `CategoryTabs` (never a hand-rolled pill row). The tabs
pin under the bar on scroll and the active role lives in the route. With tabs
present, **every** tab — authorship included — is its own narrowed read
`?role=<key>`, so the server decides which books hold a role. With one role or
none, the grid is the unnarrowed read exactly as before.

## Key decisions

- **A client-side `role === null` filter for the Author tab was tried and
  abandoned. Do not re-introduce it.** The justification for it was false: I
  believed `role=author` would be refused for an Author holding no books in that
  role, but the tab only exists once the read has answered an authorship role.
  The filter drew three P2s from the correctness axis — a book counted under
  authorship but labelled otherwise on its card appeared on no tab at all; an
  Author with "Author (2)" / "Translator (300)" stranded the reader behind a
  useless "Load more books"; and filtering the "More by" row could empty it,
  and `BookDetailRow` returns `null` for no items, so the row and its only
  heading-link back to the Author vanished.
- `mobile/components/books/BookDetailRows.tsx` is deliberately back at
  `origin/main`: slice D was reverted and the row draws the whole first page.
- The server answers `roles: []` with a **200** on a failed credits call
  (`src/app/api/v1/books/authors/[id]/route.ts:104-115`), so the screen keeps the
  last roles it held — a refetch cannot take the tabs away mid-session.
- A narrowed page always sets every card's `role` to `null` (`route.ts:168`), so a
  fixture putting a role on a narrowed card asserts an impossible answer. One
  such test was deleted rather than rewritten.
- `AUTHORSHIP_ROLE_KEY` moved to `packages/shared/src/book-author.ts`; the web
  read re-exports it.
- **Stated trade in the PR body:** AC5's "without a new read" is only partly met.
  The screen opens warm (header, facts, tabs) but the Author tab's grid costs one
  narrowed request.
