## The bug both reviewers found is the whole lesson

The tool dated each credential by `patreon_link.updated_at`: at or after
containment meant rotated, exit 0. **`src/app/api/patreon/webhook/route.ts`
bumps `updated_at` on every pledge event without touching a credential.** One
webhook after containment and the tool would have attested a rotation that
never happened — and the runbook's `delete` predicate, sharing the column,
would have spared the same row. A green tool over a live exposed secret.

The fix is `linked_at`, written only by the link callback in the same statement
as the credential. It over-reports (the tier-refresh job rewrites a token
without re-linking, so a refreshed credential still reads `stale`) and that is
the right direction: a false `stale` costs one re-link, a false `rotated` costs
an exposure nobody notices.

**The generalisable part: an `updated_at` column dates the row, not the field
you care about.** Before gating anything on a timestamp, grep every writer of
that column. I did not, and I wrote the brief.

## The redactor eats its own evidence, again

`security/harness/redact` erases any key ending in `accesstoken` or
`refreshtoken`. My `PatreonLinkRow.hasAccessToken` normalises to
`hasaccesstoken`, so the filed `report.json` would have carried
`"[redacted]"` for the exact boolean the bundle exists to record.

`scripts/verify-deployed-security.ts:73-83` **documents hitting this same trap**
with its column-keyed counts, and I read that file. Reading the sibling was not
enough; both review axes caught it independently.

The fields are now `stores…Credential`, and there is a test that runs the real
redactor over a row and asserts the booleans survive — plus one asserting they
*would* have been erased under the obvious names. Assert the effect, not the
naming rule.

manufactures its own findings. Now a CTE over the deleted rows.

## Gotchas

  Vercel `Account is blocked` billing state that fails on every PR here. It
  wants `LOGGD_MERGE_RED=1` said out loud. Confirm the red is account-wide
