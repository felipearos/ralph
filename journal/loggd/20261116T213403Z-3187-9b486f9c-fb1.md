## The unit, and why the bucket was wrong about it

`isOversize` counts surfaces by regex over the whole body. #3187 says "Mobile" (mobile),
"browser" (web) and "mobile API" (api), so it scored three surfaces with seven acceptance
criteria, under the eight threshold. By the project rule — more than 8 criteria, or three or
more surfaces actually crossed — it is a one-surface ticket. The mapper bounded it at 3–5
files, all under `mobile/` plus an optional shared URL builder: a new
`mobile/components/books/BookWhereToGetSection.tsx` modelled on `TitleExternalLinks.tsx:33`,
one insertion in `mobile/app/book/[id]/index.tsx`, and tests. Nothing like it exists
(`openlibrary`, `worldcat`, `bookshop` match nothing under `mobile/`, `packages/` or `src/`).

**This is the second lexical false positive in this bucket worth recording.** #3128 (split
earlier today) genuinely crossed api/web/mobile; this one did not. Future iterations taking
an `oversize` unit should check the surface count against the text before planning a split —
the regexes hit "mobile API" and "browser" in a pure-mobile ticket. The only seam here was a
~30-line URL builder with no consumer of its own, which fails the "a child that still needs
its parent's text is not a child" test.

## The one thing a coder must not do on reflex

The ticket says each link "opens in the device's browser, leaving the app", twice. That is
`Linking.openURL`. Every existing mobile call site uses `WebBrowser.openBrowserAsync`
(`TitleExternalLinks.tsx:33`, `WhereToWatchSection.tsx:66`, `app/settings/index.tsx:112`),
which is Custom Tabs / `SFSafariViewController` and does NOT leave the app. The convention
exists because a Loggd-owned URL handed to the system bounces back into the app
(`mobile/app/web-link.tsx:13`), which does not apply to openlibrary.org, worldcat.org or
bookshop.org. The comment says this, and says to spy on `Linking.openURL` in the test.

AC5 ("a failure to open the browser changes nothing on screen") is an absence assertion: the
comment asks for a mutation check, because it passes equally when the section renders nothing.
