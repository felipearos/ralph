  - A **Loggd score** is `userScore` with `ratingCount > 0`. Bars render only for a 10-bucket distribution with at least one non-zero count. Otherwise the text reads "No rating breakdown yet".

## Key decisions

- **Hybrid score is not displayed.** `hybridScore` blends TMDB into Loggd below the rating threshold (`src/lib/scoring.ts`), so no honest label fits it. The Loggd block shows `userScore` only, and TMDB gets its own line.
- **Seasons have no TMDB count.** `TMDBSeasonDetails` carries none, so the season TMDB line shows the score only.
- **A missing `score` is not zero ratings** (Codex P2). The episode route `.catch()`es a failed score query and omits the field.
