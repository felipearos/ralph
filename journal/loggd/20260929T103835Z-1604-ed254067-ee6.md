## Why it is blocked

AC5 requires a before/after bundle measurement for `/show/[id]` using #1586's
method: `bun run build` at `origin/main` and at this head, then the union of
root main files + root-layout entry chunks + route entry chunks from each
client-reference manifest, raw and `gzip -9`. §4 forbids heavy local builds
while Buildkite works this host, and the host was busy at **every** check of
the iteration: 08:47, 08:49, 08:52, 08:57, 09:59, 10:10, 10:36. At the last
check: 1 GB free of 31, 20 GB swap in use, builds 2626/2627 live.

No host-free route exists. The Vercel preview 302s to SSO and the apex domains
do not resolve from this shell; the Buildkite agent workspaces' `.next` dirs are
other branches' commits and in use; and no repo script implements the method
(`client-reference-manifest` appears nowhere in `scripts/`, `docs/`,
`.buildkite/`). AC5 is a hard acceptance criterion, so it cannot be waived —
and the number must not be invented.
