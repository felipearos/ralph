## #2700 → `ready-for-human` (readiness re-check, rule 5)

§2 says to re-check readiness on every ready ticket I touch, whatever the bucket, and #2700 failed
it twice over:

1. **It needs a migration.** `account_deletion_job` (`src/server/db/schema.ts:1876-1883`) has
   `user_id`, `created_at`, `attempted_at`, `claimed_at` and nothing else; `claimed_at` is the
   10-minute processing lease and `attempted_at` the last attempt, so neither can hold the
   `scheduled_for` deadline criterion 2 asks for. That is a change to `src/server/db/schema.ts` plus
   a new `drizzle/0075_*.sql` and the `supabase/migrations/` mirror. Loggd's readiness section is
   explicit and stricter than the generic safe-migration path, so it is a person's.
2. **A product question no documented rule settles.** Criterion 2 wants a cancel endpoint and a
   settings control *while the deletion fence still refuses writes*. Today an accepted request ends
   access at once — `readAccountAccess` (`src/server/queries/account-access.ts:34-55`) marks the
   account `closed` as soon as a job row exists, and the SQL predicate
   `public.account_accepts_requests()` (`drizzle/0051_lock_account_deletions.sql`) refuses every
   authenticated read and write on the same condition. For 72 hours the member can reach neither the
   cancel endpoint nor the control. Either the cancel path is exempted from the fence (a *second*
   migration, the predicate being in SQL) or "refuses writes" narrows to writes only. `docs/adr/`
   holds one unrelated ADR (`0001-setup-guarantees-a-non-empty-library.md`) and `CONTEXT.md` says
   nothing about grace periods or cancellation — its nearest entry, Password Assurance (`:314-316`),
   covers replacing a password, not deleting an account. **No rule to cite, so no decision from me**;
   `decisions` in the plan is therefore empty.
