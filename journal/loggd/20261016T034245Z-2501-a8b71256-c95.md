## The real find: "neither Storybook nor a Vitest render can mount it" reads as "criterion 5 is impossible"

Criterion 5 is the only criterion that proves `/feed` reads the *real* flag rather than a hard-coded `false`.
The `What to build` section says `HomeEmptyState` is an async server component that "neither Storybook nor a
Vitest render can mount as it stands" — true of `@testing-library/react`, false of this repo at large:
`src/app/_components/authenticatedHome.test.tsx`, `src/app/lists/popular/[[...slug]]/page.test.tsx` and
`src/app/lists/recent/page.test.tsx` all render async server components under `// @vitest-environment node`
with `renderToReadableStream`. A coder reading the note broadly drops criterion 5 or downgrades it to a
source-text assertion, and the wiring goes unproven.

Appended four criteria naming the mechanism, after **two `review.spec` rounds** that between them fixed more
than the original find:

- Round 1: pointing at `authenticatedHome.test.tsx` unqualified would have the coder copy that file's
  never-settling `forever()` mocks — the opposite of what this test needs, since the greeting renders only
  *after* `HomeEmptyState`'s whole `Promise.all` (`:122-131`) settles. Round 1 also caught that my scoping
  criterion covered only the non-pending half of criterion 1, and that `FeedContent.tsx`'s cited range cut off
  the closing `)}`.
- Round 2: three more hazards on that path, all verified by hand afterwards — an empty `getCachedBackgrounds`
  stub falls through to `getBackdropFromTrending` (`:133-135`) and a 3s `withRetry` against real TMDB; the
  inline `"use cache: remote"` helpers call `cacheLife`/`cacheTag` uncompiled under Vitest and need
  `vi.mock("next/cache")` (precedent `src/app/_loaders/homeTmdb.cache.test.ts:47`); and `FeedContent` as the
  read site needs `followingCount` to be **0** or `HomeEmptyState` never mounts. Round 2 also fixed four line
  citations of mine — `forever` is at `:269` not `:262`, `renderUntil` at `:282-312` not `:275-310`.
- The jsdom/node split became a criterion of its own: `vitest.config.ts:7` defaults to `jsdom` and
  `@vitest-environment` is per file, so criterion 4's RTL mount cannot share a file with criterion 5.
- The last appended criterion scopes criterion 1 in **both** directions. Onboarding has no follow step
  (`ONBOARDING_STEPS` is `["media", "profile", "import"]`, `src/lib/onboarding.ts:25`) so a freshly-set-up
  member does hit the empty state, but a *pending* member who follows someone before dismissing gets no
  greeting either, and criterion 1 as written still demanded one.
