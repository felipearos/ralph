## What the change does

`hydrateReviews` (`src/server/queries/review-hydration.ts`) is the shared hydrator behind the
home popular rail, the landing showcase, `/reviews`, and the v1 `reviews/popular` +
`reviews/friends` routes. It never read `movie.adult`, so a review of an adult movie reached
every viewer regardless of their preference. It now takes a required
`viewer: Pick<User, "id"> | null` (poster size moved to third) and drops a flagged movie
review unless the viewer opted in; anonymous viewers are always gated. The preference is
resolved lazily and once, via `resolveShowAdultContent`, logging before it fails closed. All
five call sites were wired.

## Key decisions

- **Required, not optional, `viewer` param** so a new caller cannot skip the gate by omission.
- **No backfill.** The shortfall (a gated review shrinks the rail rather than being replaced)
  is accepted and documented at the function and in the PR body.
- **AC5 already held**: showcase hydration runs outside the `"use cache: remote"` wrappers; a
  comment now pins that so a later refactor cannot quietly move it inside.
- **Declined:** `isTitleVisibleToViewer` (it takes an already-resolved boolean, which defeats
  the lazy short-circuit — `hydrateMediaBasics` inlines the same check); gating TV reviews
  (out of scope, every per-title gate here is movie-only); an e2e spec.
- **Follow-up candidate:** `/feed`'s `PopularReviewsWithFriends` renders a gated review under
  "Unknown Show" rather than dropping it.
