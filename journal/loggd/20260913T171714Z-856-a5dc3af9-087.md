## Decisions for whoever touches stats next

- **Only diary-year bars link.** The watched grids (`profile/[username]/movies|shows`) have no filters. The Watchlist's genre/country/language/decade filters narrow unwatched saved titles (a different meaning), and Browse has yet another. Adding a real filtered watched library would make the other rows linkable.
- **Known parity gaps, not fixed:**
  - the diary link carries no media type (web's `/diary?year=` has the same gap);
  - the adult gate runs after the query's per-decade limits, so a decade can hold fewer than 10 titles.
- **Adding a section** is not yet one-file. Every section is a hook, a component, and an entry in `patternQueries`. A deferred section belongs in `breakdownQueries`, with `pendingUntilRevealed` and `{ enabled: breakdownsRequested }`.
- **Declined review smells:** a shared segmented control (it would rework the existing scope control), a shared stats test-fixture helper across three files, and one table for the By Year mode switches.
