## What changed
GET /api/v1/lists/[id] now answers a book List's In-List Controls:
- `q` searches title and authors.
- Sort options are added, release, title and rating.
- Genre uses the Book Browse Catalog through getBookShelfMatchIds.
- Decade reads `release_year`.

Each entry also carries its curator's `creatorReview`, read through the new getBatchBookReviewDetails, which returns published reviews only. `itemCount` is the total and `matchedCount` is the filtered count. Progress still covers the whole List and no longer waits on the curator markers. CONTEXT.md gained the term "In-List Controls".

## Decisions
- An unknown book genre or decade is dropped, matching show Lists.
- Each sort has one direction, matching show Lists.
- `creatorReview` is optional on the shared type.
- A failed genre read returns no match and is logged.
- LIST_CONTROL_KEYS is typed as Exclude<keyof ListControls,"book">.
- List controls read only the string-valued keys from search params.
