## The decision that matters for the next split

**I split by rule, not by surface, and the reason is mechanical.** Loggd's
triage rule says to take the surface seam first. A surface split here (shared,
then web, then mobile, then the cores) makes the client children depend on the
shared child landing first — and `triage-apply.ts` cannot express that: only
`add-blocker`'s `blockedBy` may name a plan-local `ref`, never its `issue`, so
there is no way to write a blocking edge between two issues one plan creates.
Those edges would have existed only as prose the frontier does not read, and
coders would have been handed the client children before the session owned the
rules. The three rules are disjoint at line level (the mapper checked: no two
touch the same function), so the rule seam needs no such edge and each child
lands alone in any order. **Next triager hitting a tree-shaped oversize ticket:
check for child→child ordering before choosing the surface seam.**

#2729, #2730 and #2732 share `packages/shared/src/list-edit-session.ts` and its
test file in different functions, so they share one staffing group rather than
running in parallel. #2731 touches only the two write cores and got its own.

## The surface-count trap

`isOversize` in `frontier/next-tasks.ts` counts the body's matches of
`\bweb\b|\bbrowser\b`, `\bmobile\b|\bios\b|\bandroid\b`,
`\bapi\b|\bendpoint\b|\bserver\b` and `\bdatabase\b|\bschema\b|\bmigration\b`,
and three or more re-offers the ticket as oversize. **A `src/server/...` path in
a child body counts as a surface**, which is how a child of a split gets
re-split forever. So #2729 paraphrases the downstream write path rather than
citing it, and the core coupling is its own child naming no client. I grepped
the four published bodies with those exact patterns afterwards:
`#2729 6/2`, `#2730 7/2`, `#2731 8/1`, `#2732 8/2` (criteria/surfaces), all
under both thresholds.

## Two places the children go past #2676's own wording

1. #2676's first criterion says "mobile unchanged". #2729 does change mobile:
   `canSave` at `mobile/app/list/[id]/manage.tsx:663-672` requires
   `rows.length > 0`, so mobile today empties a List and then refuses to save
   anything, a rename included. #2676 also asks that the `canSave`/empty rules
   derive from the session, so leaving the term in would make the rule true on
   one client only. Stated plainly in the triage comment and in #2729's body, as
   the thing to drop if it is the wrong call.
2. #2731 exempts a non-owner from the coupling. A Collaborator reaches
   `updateListContentCore` by restating access policy unchanged
   (`src/server/queries/list-content-write.ts:397-420`), so on a public List
   whose link is on, their save would otherwise switch the owner's link off.
   Decided from a cited rule (`CONTEXT.md`, the **Collaborator** entry: "An
   accepted Collaborator may curate the List's contents and presentation but
   never its List Audience, its List Discussion, its background or its
   deletion — those stay the owner's"), carried in the plan's `decisions`.

#2731 also states what "couple" covers: the audience and `share_enabled` only.
An existing `share_token` is not cleared, and public rows already carrying
`share_enabled = true` are not backfilled; only minting a *new* token for a
public List stops.

## The spec review earned its keep

`ralph-review-spec` found three blocking defects in my first draft, all of them
things a coder would have hit mid-ticket:

1. Dropping `isCreationEmpty`'s `defaultVisibility` argument (which #2676 asks
   for as "one shared rule") would make an untouched
   `/list/new?visibility=friends` form non-empty and write a List Draft the
   moment it opened — `useListCreateSession.ts:371` passes `initialVisibility`
   today. The parameter stays, defaulting to `emptyListEditState().visibility`.
2. Mobile manage must send `shareEnabled: false` on the wire, not only stage it:
   `ListSettingChange` (`mobile/hooks/useManageList.ts:203-206`) is a one-field
   union, so a coupled session would show the link off and a reload would bring
   it back. That is also what makes #2730 standalone rather than secretly
   dependent on #2731.
3. Mobile creation's `canSave` cannot take its item check from
   `isCreationEmpty`: the create body requires at least one item
   (`packages/shared/src/schemas.ts:293`), so a name-only draft would be
   let through and lose its `ITEMS_REQUIRED` message. Out of #2732, stated as
   out of scope.

It also caught that `mobile/app/list/create.tsx:620` is not where the name/item
checks live (they are in `handleSave` at `:627-635`), that
`useListEditSession.test.tsx:162-180` tests `"friends"` and says nothing about
the public send, that `list-edit-session.test.ts:510` is the Collaborator table
and the immediate-op cases are at `:268`, and four more off-by-a-few citations.
All corrected before the write. Worth paying for the fetched-spec brief: every
finding was anchored on real line numbers.
