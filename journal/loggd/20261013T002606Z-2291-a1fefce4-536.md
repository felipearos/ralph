`router` for `usePressInPreparation` on two targets: the artwork button and the
episode-name link prepare the episode screen, the show-name link prepares the show's

## Key decisions

- The episode snapshot keeps `name` on both kinds of row; only `stillThumbUrl` is
  withheld. AC1's "hands none" means no *still* (issue body: "hands over no still").
  Both review axes confirmed that reading.
- The show snapshot omits `posterThumbUrl` and `year` deliberately: the issue asks for
  name and poster path, and the row carries no show air date. Both axes called the
  omission a non-defect, consistent with `ListEntryRow`'s text pressable.
- The two screen tests stub `useQueryClient` — the established pattern in
  `CatchUpRow.test.tsx` and four episode/season tests — rather than wrapping a real
  `QueryClientProvider`, whose client would attempt the deliberately-mocked reads.
- AC2's "prepares the episode screen with the card's still" is already covered by
  `components/__tests__/CatchUpRow.test.tsx:77`, which exercises the **home carousel**
  `CatchUpCard` via `hooks/usePrepareEpisode.ts` — a different component. Left
  byte-untouched; a future iteration should not "fix" it.
- Episode reads are counted by spying on `prefetchQuery`, on purpose: it catches a
  second prepare on press, which a deduped network count cannot. The show case counts
  at `api.get`. Raised as a standards P3 and answered rather than changed.
