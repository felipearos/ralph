## The review's one blocking finding, and why no product decision was needed

`ralph-review-spec` read the fetched spec (`.ralph/logs/spec-2961.md`, re-fetched this iteration) and found criteria 1–4 and 7 testable, 6 loose, 5 needing a sentence, and **8 self-contradictory**: it says the web callers of `buildProfileReviewConditions` "keep their launch-constant behaviour" while the body says they "keep their current behaviour". Those cannot both hold — the launch-constant set excludes books, and current behaviour includes them. I verified it: the one web caller, `ReviewsContent.tsx:50-55`, passes `mediaFilter = "all"`, which builds no condition, so the web profile reviews page **shows book reviews today**.

This looked like a product question and is not one. The body's own fence decides it: `src/app/[username]/**` is out of this diff, so the call site cannot change, so current behaviour is what holds. And #2966 already owns the web gate by its own text — it claims `src/app/[username]/(profile)/reviews/page.tsx` and says "Do not touch `src/server/queries/{profile-reviews,…}.ts`". So the answer came from the tree's declared ownership, not from my judgement of what the product should do, and needed no `decisions` citation.

It has a shape consequence I wrote down because a coder would otherwise discover it the hard way: the viewer set must reach `buildProfileReviewConditions` as an **optional** field on `ProfileReviewScope`. A required field forces an edit at the web call site, which this ticket may not make.
