with more than 8 acceptance-criteria checkboxes **or** three or more of `SURFACES` (:1202) matched

behaviour as web" — a parity statement), and *server* ("gated by the server's rule" — a rule it reads,
does not implement). A false positive, and worth knowing that this detector cannot distinguish a
surface a ticket BUILDS from one it CONSUMES. Expect more of these in the #2478 tree, where every
child cross-references the tracer.
