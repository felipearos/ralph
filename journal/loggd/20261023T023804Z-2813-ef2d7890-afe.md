## What I decided and why

The unit is the same incident as **#2812**, which this same loop triaged to
`ready-for-agent` ~50 minutes earlier in this run. `learnings/loggd.md` carries
the 2026-10-12 Vercel outage under **four** slugs — `vercel-status-not-posted`
(#2812), `vercel-status-not-posting` (#2813), `vercel-status-missing-on-pr-head`
(no ticket) and `vercel-posts-no-pr-status` (#2814, still `needs-triage`) — and
the clerk filed one `learning-fix` ticket per slug. They differ only in which
PRs they name.

All of them rest on one false premise: *a missing `Vercel` status holds the PR,
because the `Protect main` ruleset requires `Vercel`.* It has not since
2026-10-13. #2812's acceptance criteria are precisely the fix for that premise
in this repo, and they cover #2813 point for point:

- AC1/AC2 remove the two tracked sentences that still claim it (`CLAUDE.md:45`,
  `docs/dependency-policy.md:207`);
- AC3/AC4 add the mutation-checked drift case in
  `scripts/agent-instructions.test.ts` tying `ruleset requires` to
  `.ralph/config.json`'s `requiredChecks`, so it cannot come back under any
  slug;
- AC5 records in `docs/merge-gates.md` that a missing `Vercel` status does not
  hold a PR — which is the whole of #2813's "Current workaround".

#2813's own criteria are the filer's placeholders ("specify a reproducible
verification criterion before marking this ready"), so it left nothing unbuilt.
Specifying it independently would have handed two coders the identical
four-file diff.
