on book covers, the long-press sheet every cover opens, and Fade Watched for read books,
everywhere a book cover is drawn. It has 37 native sub-issues, 8 closed and **29 open

## The one split I kept despite a flagged risk, and the fence I wrote instead

`book-viewer-marks` and `book-cover-sheet` could collide on
`mobile/components/books/BookCard.tsx`: #3164/#3167 could add a marks opt-out prop there
rather than in the Context, and #3159 could change how `BookCard` feeds
`useBookCoverLongPress`. Merging them would have made a 14-ticket serial group — half the
spec — so instead the comment states the fence as a coder instruction: **the marks
projection stays in `BookViewerStateContext.tsx` (`ratingOnly()` is already there) and the
long-press snapshot stays in `useBookCoverLongPress.tsx`, so `BookCard.tsx` belongs to
neither group.** Second fence, same shape: the eight `book-longpress-surfaces` children all
say "through the shared book cover outside a provider (#3156)", so they *call*
`useBookCoverLongPress` and must not widen it; a surface drawing `BookCover` directly wraps
it locally.

If a coder ignores either fence, the groups are wrong and a rebase is the cost. Worth
re-reading before the next pass on this tree.
