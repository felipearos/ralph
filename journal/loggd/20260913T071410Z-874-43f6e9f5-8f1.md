## Follow-up worth a ticket

- **`mobile/hooks/useFeedControls.ts` may have the same queued-write hole.** Its mutations close over the render-time `viewerId`, both for `expectedUserId` and inside the scope. Its scope id and mutation key are keyed by `viewerId`, so an account switch may reset the observer (a `mutationKey` hash change calls `reset()`) instead of copying options onto the pending mutation. That's likely safe, but unverified. Check it before reusing that pattern with a scope shared across accounts.
