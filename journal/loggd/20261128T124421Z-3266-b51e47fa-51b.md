## Key decisions
- A series notification wins over an author notification only when the series bell switch is on.
- The run-end send goes out in chunks of at most 1000 that never split a member, and it runs before the prune. A handle failure in one batch does not cancel the send.
- Each series position gets one entry (the featured one, or else the lowest id). A position that was already held does not count as new, which trades off placeholder replacement.
- Dropped: dedupe of author and series notifications across runs, because the spec only asks for it within a run.
