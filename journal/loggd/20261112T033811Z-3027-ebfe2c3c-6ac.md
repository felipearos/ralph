## What the frontier handed me

`--take-one --role triager` with the plan's nine exclusions returned #3027
("Spec: Read and Wantlist marks on book covers in the app") in the `staffing`

## Why no plan could do it

Staffing is all-or-nothing — `frontier/next-tasks.ts:975-982` puts a tree in
`legacyTrees` if **any** non-spec child has `staffingGroup === null`, so a
partially annotated tree behaves exactly like an unannotated one. #3027 has
**35 open non-spec children** (#3032 #3033 #3035 #3075 #3076 #3156 #3158 #3159
#3160 #3162 #3163 #3164 #3165 #3166 #3167 #3169 #3171 #3173 #3176 #3247 #3251
#3368–#3381), none of them a container, none of them already grouped, plus the
closed #3030 and #3031.

`plannedWrites` counts one write per op (`triage/order.ts:89`), so the minimum
legal plan — one `set-section` per child, no comment, no labels — is 35 writes
against `RALPH_TRIAGE_MAX_WRITES` default **30** (`triage/validate.ts:60`). A
dry run of exactly that plan returned one refusal and only one:

```
triage-apply: refused, nothing written
  - plan: 35 mutations exceeds RALPH_TRIAGE_MAX_WRITES (30)
```

The plan is otherwise well-formed, so the cap is the whole obstacle. Dropping
the triage comment leaves 35 > 30. Splitting across runs is out of bounds, and
a 30-of-35 plan would spend the budget to change nothing while leaving a marker
that makes the tree look handled.

## What I did instead

Posted the claim comment, then the `blocked:` comment above (it carries the
child list, the refusal, and the two ways forward), pushed a high-priority
`ticket-blocked` notification, removed `tria-wip`, added `tria-blocked`, and
released the lease by hand (`claim-lease.ts release 3027` — it hit "lease store
was locked" once, retried clean). `tria-blocked` on a spec does not count as a
claim (`isClaimed` reads `-wip`, assignees, open PRs and live branches), and
#3027 is not `ready-for-agent`, so none of the 35 children is hidden from the
coders by this. No child was written to.

## The two ways forward, both a person's

1. Raise `RALPH_TRIAGE_MAX_WRITES` to >= 36 for one triager run.
2. Restructure #3027. 35 leaf children under one spec is past what any single
   staffing plan can annotate; 29 of them are the `needs-info` block from #3158
   on, which reads like several specs filed as one (the long-press sheet, Fade
   Watched, the per-screen marks). Sub-specs would each be a staffable tree.

## For the next iteration
