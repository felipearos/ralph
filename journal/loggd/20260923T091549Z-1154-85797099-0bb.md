- Rule recorded on CONTEXT.md "Scoped Log": deleting a log never changes watch status by itself. When the deleted log is the last non-draft log at a watched/caught-up Media Scope, the confirmation offers "Also mark as unwatched", unticked by default. A rewatch never gets the offer.
