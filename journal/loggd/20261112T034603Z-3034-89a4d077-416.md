### The one judgement call

**#3157 went to `list-share`, not `link-routing`.** It applies the Books-mode
switch when a List is opened from outside the app, so it reads as routing — but
its edit lands on `mobile/app/list/[id]/index.tsx`, which #3161 also edits to add
Share, and its own body says the List destination already resumes after sign-in
and only the switch is added (so it does not touch `pending-intent.ts`). Put in
`link-routing` it would have collided with `list-share` across a group boundary,
which is the thing groups exist to prevent.
