## What landed

`src/app/feed/feed-section-plan.ts`'s `readWebFeedSectionPlan` reads all five Feed
Sections once through `readFeedSectionBatch` and plans them with the shared
`planFeedSections`. `src/app/feed/FeedSections.tsx` renders each plan entry through
`src/components/feed/FeedTitlesSection | FeedReviewsSection | FeedListsSection`,
which take a plan entry and hold no label or empty copy of their own. The five old
self-fetching components plus `FriendActivityCard` and `PopularWithFriends` are
deleted (-1733 lines). `FeedSectionListItem.owner` gained `id`; the entry types and
`WebFeedSectionPlan` live in the plain module `src/lib/feed-section-entry.ts`.

## Key decisions

- The web feed now renders WIRE items: friend-activity richness (author, rating,
  episode), the Popular-with-Friends counts and the review-number badge are gone from
  the feed. Intended — the ticket requires the shared envelope — and called out in the
  PR body.
- Titles sections no longer gate on preferences (they answer to no category), so a
  member with every category switched off now sees them.
- List labels are the shared ones; both List sections share a "Browse lists"
  more-link; the titles sections have none.
- `renderedFallbackSources`: only a fallback that actually rendered titles stands
  movie mode's Trending row down. Both review axes found the looser rule; the mixed
  case is tested in both orderings.
- The three committed `e2e/poster-sizing.spec.ts-snapshots/feed-{375,768,1280}-chromium-authenticated-linux.png`
  baselines were replaced. That is part of the ticket's visible change, not a
  workaround: the feed route is exactly what the baseline captures. Poster and card
  geometry — what #471 put the baseline there to guard — is unchanged; only page
  height moved (2115→2336 at 1280, 2104→2136 at 768, 2402→2457 at 375).
- Declined and recorded: moving the `renderedFallbackSources` rule into shared
  `planFeedSections` (mobile's `FeedTab` has the same latent case — follow-up under
  #2016/#2017's ground); a "serial" `getBlockedUserIds` (its result is the batch's
  input); replacing the hand-written `sections:` map with a loop (needs an `as`).
- No `CONTEXT.md` change: the glossary's Feed Section entry already states these rules.
