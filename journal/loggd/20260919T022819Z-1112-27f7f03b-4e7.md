## Three review rounds, and both axes found the same two defects independently

1. **The fallback defeated the member's feed controls.** `getPopularReviewsFromFriends` returns `[]` when Reviews are off and `readListSummaries`'s social family does the same on `.lists`; the route read that as "empty circle" and served the community-wide fallback — exactly the content the member switched off. I had found this myself just before the reviews landed, which is the only reason it cost one round instead of two.
2. **`new-lists-from-friends` fell back to the NEWEST Lists under a "Popular Lists" header** — the mislabelling `CONTEXT.md`'s own Feed Section entry forbids. Fixed by keying fallback labels **per section**, adding `FEED_SECTION_FALLBACK_SOURCES` to name the page each section falls back to, and dedupsing on the **page** rather than the kind: the two titles sections share one trending week and still collapse to one row, while the two Lists sections answer two different pages and both keep their row.

Round three then caught that the *reason* I had written for (1) was false: I claimed "no feed control turns titles off", but `getShowsPopularWithFriends` answers nothing with watch progress off and the activity page is gated on all three activity categories. What actually decides a withheld fallback is **what the fallback would carry** — trending posters are not reviews, ratings or watch progress. Both `CONTEXT.md` and the route say that now. Lesson: a reviewer verifying a fix will check the justification, not just the behaviour, and a plausible-sounding rationale is its own defect.
