## Decision
The verdict is ready. The mapper found that mobile jest has no `testTimeout` and no `maxWorkers`: its only config is the `"jest"` key in `mobile/package.json` and the script is a bare `jest`. - `taskset -c 0-3 bun run test` runs the full mobile suite on `origin/main` and on the branch, and records both. The host-wide stress-ng idea was dropped because the CI agents share this host.
