## What landed
When a check-in is due, the sentinel gate leases it and gathers the evidence into the watch store: PR files and routes, the other PRs in the same deploy (kept as a superset), and Sentry health and traffic via sentinel/sentry.ts. The store holds leases plus evidence and decision records, and the heartbeat renews leases through role-leases.ts. The session works one check-in with `sentinel/checkin.ts show|decide`. decideVerdict is forced to observe until #567/#568, so nothing is written to the tracker. The sentinel prompt was rewritten, with a matching prompt-render test.

## Key decisions
- The Sentry token stays with the gate only, so the gate gathers the evidence and the session only reads it.
- otherPrs is a superset: an extra PR can only turn a revert into a ticket, never the other way.
- A PR with no routes is judged on health alone. With the floor on, it ends inconclusive, not verified. A check-in with no evidence is recorded inconclusive and the watch stays open.
