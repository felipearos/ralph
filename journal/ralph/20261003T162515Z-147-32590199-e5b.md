**Key decisions.**

- **Every delegated route was down all iteration — six failures.** `delegate.sh`
  ×1 (executor, `implement`), `inspect.sh` mapper ×2 (`select`), review.standards
  ×2 and review.spec ×2 (`review`); all exit 4 `runner-error (post-hoc)` with
  empty logs. Route 3 (in-session `Agent`, opus) carried the mapping, both review
  axes and both review rounds, and the slices were written in session. Every
  slice was delegable — A and C especially — and the briefs survive at
  `.ralph/logs/brief-147-*.md` for whoever gets the runners back. This is the
  same opencode outage the #140/#142/#145/#146 journals record; it is now the
  normal shape of an iteration rather than an incident, and it is the single
  largest cost multiplier on this backlog.
- **Re-reviewing a review's fixes earned its keep.** Round 1 found 4 must-fix
  defects, two of them in the branch's headline feature: AC 4 was not actually
  met (`--role` never reached `--validate`, so the form wrote three stages and
  was refused for "missing 'executor'" — and the fix needs the loop IDENTITY
  too, because `rolesForValidation` keys the role by `RALPH_PROJECT_ID` /
  `RALPH_LOOP_NAME`), and a triager configured before roles existed could not
  open the form at all. Round 2 then found holes in *those* fixes: the fallback
  reported the CODER's refusal for a block neither role accepts, and a null cap
  blanked a knowable number.
- **Three deliberate deviations from the issue**, all in the PR body and raised
  on the issue (comment 5923085198): no `rescue` bucket (four exist; `rescue`
  deferred to #150/#155), `RALPH_TRIAGE_MAX_WRITES` is a per-plan cap rather
  than the hourly budget the issue assumes, and "the current hour" is read as a
  trailing hour.
- **Accepted without change, with reasons:** `narrowSchema` does not carry its
  documented `role` field (pre-existing); `Stat` stays re-implemented in
  `triage.tsx` because sharing it needs `Tone`/`TONE_TEXT` out of `app.tsx`, a
  broad refactor with no render tests; `--dry-run --json` resolves the owner
  eagerly where the text path resolves it last (a partial JSON document would be
  worse, and the dashboard cannot reach that state).
