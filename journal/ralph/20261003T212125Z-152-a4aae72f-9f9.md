## What landed

A diff guard the ci-doctor must clear: it may make a flaky test reliable and a
slow test fast, but may not weaken a test to get green, nor touch CI
configuration. The rules live in `executor-guard/diff-guard.ts` and two callers
share them — `ci-doctor/verify.ts` for the verify phase, `guard.ts diff` for the
executor. Both exit 0 or 10.

Six commits: `a34526d` parser/glob/scope · `376f7a9` weakening rules ·
`d537f44` timeouts, the revert exception, entry point and fixtures · `e8277ce`
the two callers + README · `772afe4` round-1 review fixes · `abe9cb0` round-2.

## Key decisions

- **AC 2 is not met and cannot be as written.** The issue wants the guard to
  accept loggd #1728 while capping a test at 30 s; the real commit raises three
  `it(…)` bounds to 120 s (the two `beforeAll` hooks are fine). And #1728's PR
  body carries no measured durations to key a per-test ceiling off — it could
  not, since the walk it bounds timed out. Implemented as specified, ceilings
  made configurable, all three outcomes pinned by tests, and both decisions
  raised on the issue (comment 5973559360) as the spec owner's to make. Also
  restated in the merge comment so it is not lost with the branch.
- The rules sit in `executor-guard/` rather than `ci-doctor/` because `guard.ts`
  may import nothing from the harness; that is what makes AC 4 one
  implementation instead of two.
- `guard.ts diff` deliberately has **no `--revert`**. The exception skips the
  weakening and timeout rules, so a caller free to name the comparison clears
  any diff by naming a copy of itself — and in the sandbox that caller is the
  executor.
- `ciDoctor` is read raw from `.ralph/config.json`; `project-config.ts` would
  reject the key and #158 owns typing it. Follows `ci-doctor/gate.ts:776`.
