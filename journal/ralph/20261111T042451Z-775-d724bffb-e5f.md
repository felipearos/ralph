## Spec review (`ralph-review-spec`, two briefs)
Brief 1, the promotion of #775/#776/#777, re-read them against the real
`park-store.ts` (they were written before #774 landed). Six findings, all acted
on, every citation re-checked by hand:
1. **#776 would have been unparkable for the commonest condition.** AC2 told
   the session to run `park-store.ts check --condition <json>` with no `--pr`,
   and `validateCondition` refuses `{"kind":"check","on":"pr"}` without one
   (`park-store.ts:148`). AC2 now takes `[--pr <n>]` and requires it there.
2. **#777 AC4 was unmeetable on the route #776 offers**: it needs the condition
   that never held for an expired park, and #776 AC5 promised only summary and
   budget. #776 AC5 now carries `condition`, `expired` and `escalatedAt`.
3. **A replaced park could be deleted.** `withStore`'s critical section is
   synchronous (`park-store.ts:332-336`), so every remote read is outside the
   lock, and `unpark` keys on the ticket number alone (`:223-228`). New
   criterion on #775: the woken and escalating paths re-read `since` under the
   lock and skip a park that was replaced.
4. **The escalation body was unbuildable**: `Park` stores `summary`, not the
   block record (`:52-64`). #775's push body is the summary; reading the issue's
   comments is out of scope.
5. **Read cap hole + no interval**: the cap now counts Buildkite `step` reads
   too (`bk-log.ts:333-343`), and a park checked within
   `RALPH_PARK_MIN_INTERVAL_SECONDS` (default 120) is skipped. The sweep runs
   every iteration and every gate poll in every loop on the host, and these
   loops have been 403'd before. **My judgement call, named on #775 as the
   operator's to overrule.**
6. **No mutator for `lastChecked`/`escalatedAt`**: #775 now says they are set
   inside `withStore`'s `critical`, and any mutator added stays pure over a
   `Store`.

Two more I wrote into the bodies rather than leaving to a reviewer to relitigate:
