**What landed.** A vitest `globalSetup` guard (`test-ralph-logs.ts` +
`test-ralph-logs.global.ts`) snapshots `<repo>/.ralph` and the tracked
`fixtures/loggd/.ralph`, then fails the run naming anything a suite wrote
there. `inspect.test.ts` was the one real offender — 1439 leaked lock files and
3783 artifacts into the checkout — and now runs against a scratch project. Four
near-miss suites were hardened, and `runner-failure.ts` resolves its project on
first use instead of at import. Six commits, `ccec2ee`..`2ee602a`.

**Key decisions.** The guard is strict only where the acceptance criterion is
judged: the local review's load-bearing finding was that the ignore list
applied with no loop identity, which left the guard weakest in CI, exactly
where AC 1 is checked. Fixed in `2ee602a`.

Relocating `inspect.sh`'s locks to a tmpdir was chosen over fixing the leak at
source: that lock *is* the exit-5 "already running" signal, so changing its
lifetime is a harness change outside this ticket.

**Delegation.** `delegate.sh` was not used — it and every `opencode` inspect
route exit 1 with an empty log on this host (see the standing note on the
opencode runners being down). The implementation and both review axes ran
in-session on opus as route 3. The brief-writing slice was the delegable one;
it was kept in-session only because no runner would take it.

**Files touched.** `test-ralph-logs.ts`, `test-ralph-logs.global.ts`,
`vitest.config.ts`, `inspect.test.ts`, `runner-failure.ts`, and four suites
adjusted off the checkout's `.ralph`.
