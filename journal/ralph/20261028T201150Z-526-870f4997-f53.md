## What
A fake `gh` (`fake-gh.ts`, the `fake-gh/gh` shim, `fake-gh.test.ts`) that #525's sandboxed trials use. It keeps a trial's PRs in a local state file. For each new head it runs a checks command and a findings command (a detached runner, with the review as a check of its own). Findings are posted as review threads that `review-threads.ts` answers, and `gh api` covers threads, replies and comments. CI is held until the review lands, and an error fails closed. Merges are refused and recorded, unsupported commands are refused, and it never runs the real gh.
