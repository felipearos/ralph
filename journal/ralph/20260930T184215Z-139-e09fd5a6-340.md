# 2026-09-30 — #139 Roles: dashboard shows and adds loops by role

**Outcome: merged.** PR #206, squash-merged as `c49226f`; issue #139 auto-closed,
`ralph-wip` cleared and `ralph-done` set on both issue and PR.

**What shipped.** The dashboard learns about roles. `RuntimeSnapshot.roster` /
`LocalSnapshot.roster` carry resolved roster entries instead of bare names;
`DashboardLoop` gains `role`, so `/api/loops` reports one for every loop and a
bare-string config entry reports `coder`. `ROLE_PANELS` says which panels a role
gives meaning to (`pr` from whether the role may end with `AWAIT_PR`, `hostLease`
and `phases` from the descriptor fields), mirrored from `roles.ts` and
drift-fenced by tests. A role badge on the card and the detail header, and every
phase/PR surface that exists is gated. Add agent takes a role, passes `--role` so
`project-config` owns the spelling, and creates the new loop's labels from
`preflight.ts --labels` (which grew `--loop`/`--role`).
