## What I decided and why

The frontier handed me spec #525 ("backtest human tickets against sandboxed coder
iterations (A/B, no PRs)") in the `staffing` bucket. Its ten open non-spec children
#526–#535 are all `ready-for-agent` and **none** of them carried a `## Staffing
group`, so the whole tree sat at its parent-wide collision domain: any two coders
taking two of these ten would have been treated as colliding.

Groups come from the files each child edits on `origin/main`, not from the
blocked-by order. The decisive fact from the mapper: **`experiments.ts` is one
429-line file** holding the CLI dispatch (`:413`), the config parser
(`parseExperiment` `:85`), the run-directory layout (`runExperiment` `:281`) and
the scorecard renderer (`renderExperiment` `:251`). There is no `experiments/`
directory and no separate report module. So every child that adds to the
`backtest` command or to the report edits that same file plus
`experiments.test.ts` (229 lines).

Why the three singletons are singletons:

- **#526** adds one new module and its own suite. Its checks command *and* its
  findings command are pluggable by design, so #531 supplies commands rather than
  editing it. No other child names those files.
- **#527** is the `local` tracker-kind prefactor. Its edits land in
  `tracker/types.ts:20` (`TrackerKind`), `tracker/index.ts:53` (`createTracker`),
  `project-config.ts:399`/`:813`, `preflight.ts:677`, `tracker/project.ts:34` and
  the dashboard connector's `KINDS` (`dashboard/ui/tracker-connector.tsx:24`).
  Nothing else in the tree goes near the tracker layer.
- **#529** adds the local Revisión Claude module. Nothing like it exists:
  `.github/workflows/` here holds only `ci.yml`, the only mention of
  `revision-claude.yml` is a comment at `review-threads.ts:61`, and Playwright is
  used solely by `ui-shots.ts`. #531 and #534 *call* this module; neither edits it.

#530 and #531 share a group because #531 is a change to the sandbox #530 builds
(point its findings command at the local review, record the rounds) and both add
cases to `end-to-end.test.ts`, which is 2320 lines today.

Where it was close I shared the group rather than split it, per §2: #534 and #535
both grow `renderExperiment`, and #528's comment-cutoff work lands in the same
`parseExperiment`/`runExperiment` that #532's config schema does.
