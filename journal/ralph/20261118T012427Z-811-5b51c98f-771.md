## What I decided and why

The learning suggested two things: (a) back off on a repeated `ALL_BLOCKED`, and (b) have the gate report "work waiting on an operator" instead of an empty frontier.

- **(a) is out of scope.** With `RALPH_WAIT_FOR_WORK` set, the gate already polls before every spawn. A refuted `ALL_BLOCKED` is already bounded by `BLOCKED_STREAK_LIMIT` and the #771 hold in `wait_or_stop`. Ungated, it stops.
- **(b) is specced.** `--take-one` reports `waitingOnOperator` counts per `HUMAN_REASONS`, using the same `humanReason()` and ownership as `frontierReport`. `frontier-gate.ts` `decide()` appends `— N waiting on an operator (...)` to `the frontier is empty`. A field that is absent, all zeros or malformed leaves the text unchanged.
- The empty verdict has no `verdictSubject`, so holds are unaffected.
- Durable state (`frontier-wait`/`frontier-empty` records) and the dashboard are a follow-up.
- The spec review found nothing blocking. I folded in its four P3 points: a pure helper as the test seam, malformed input reads as absent, `pick()` in the tests, and durable-state scope.
