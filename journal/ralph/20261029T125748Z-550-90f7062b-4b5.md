only `ralph.sh` does; the fake doppler's silent catch-all (`ralph.test.ts:258`) would have

Round 2 found two more, both real and both now in the ticket text:

1. **A credential leak I had written in.** My draft told the coder to drop the recovered
   secret's `env -u` entry from `RALPH_ENV_FILTER`. Both clerk tokens and the triager's
   Jira token are `targets: ["gate"]` (`roles.ts:545,767,771`), and `ralph.sh:1890-1895`
   gives a gate-only secret an `env -u` entry even on a clean pass — so meeting that AC
   would have handed the clerk's Slack and Jira tokens to every iteration's model session.
   I verified both lines myself before rewriting. `What to build` now says "Do not relax
   `RALPH_ENV_FILTER`" and gives the reason; AC5 pins the fake `claude`'s `clerk-tokens=`
   staying empty while the gate env carries the value.
2. **`end-to-end.test.ts` cannot cover this.** Its `ralph.sh` is a stand-in that only
   keeps a process alive (`:430-438`) and it does not run `preflight.ts` at all, for the
   reason its own header gives (`:22-26`). AC6 now says the `ralph.test.ts` cases carry
   `CLAUDE.md`'s shared-lifecycle rule, and why, and asks for an `end-to-end.test.ts`
   case only if the change adds a new `loop-state` event.

Its two non-blocking points were folded in as well: AC4 asserts no second `secrets get`
for an already-available name (the fake logs every call to `argv.log`,
`ralph.test.ts:233,240`), and `What to build` says a value that re-reads but fails its
check goes to `RALPH_ROLE_SECRETS_WITHHELD` and so stays `denied`.
