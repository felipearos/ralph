## What shipped

`--take-one --role triager` now hands over a UNIT (a bucket plus its tickets)
from the first non-empty of four buckets — staffing, routing, intake, oversize —
instead of always a single ticket. `roleCandidates` became `roleUnits`; `takeOne`
walks units and claims as much of one as it can get inside the existing
`withClaimLock`. New `triage-marker.ts` is the record a triager leaves on an
issue, so a decision that still stands keeps that issue out of the intake bucket.
The census reads `author` and the last 20 comments in the same GraphQL call, and
`Ticket` keeps its `body`. `bucket`/`tickets` appear in the JSON only for a
non-`ready` unit, so the coder role's output stays byte-identical.
