## Why the label flip is the substance of this iteration

The rescue bucket's filter (`frontier/next-tasks.ts:1350-1364`) requires
`READY_LABEL`. So while #383 stayed `ready-for-agent` + `ralph-blocked`, it was
**re-offerable to the triager on every iteration**, indefinitely — the same
shape of livelock the block record documents on the coder side, where two
`ralph` iterations an hour apart each re-derived this answer at ~1M
orchestrator tokens. Taking `ready-for-agent` off and putting `ready-for-human`
on removes it from the coder frontier and from the rescue bucket in one move.

`ralph-blocked` was deliberately left in place. It is not mine to clear outside
a granted rescue, `remove-label` refuses every `*-blocked` anyway, and it costs
nothing: it is what keeps the ticket on the dashboard's needs-you list. The
ticket now carries exactly `ralph-blocked, ready-for-human`.

No `ralph-review-spec` ran and none was owed: the plan writes no section and
promotes nothing to `ready-for-agent`. No mapper call was needed either — four
`git grep`s over `origin/main` and two `gh pr view --json files` answered every
question, which is the cheap check the previous iterations' journals
recommended.
