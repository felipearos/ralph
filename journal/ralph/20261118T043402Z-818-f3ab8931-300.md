## What changed
- `scoring.judge.contested` takes `"inversion"` (the default, today's rule) or `{spread: N}` (0 < N <= 4). Under spread, an arm is contested on an axis when its families' mean scores differ by at least N (with a 1e-9 tolerance).
- PanelResult carries `rule` (when it is absent the rule is inversion). `Contested.arms` is now `ArmId[]`, because a spread contest involves only one arm.
- report.md and the Evals view name the rule: "Contested (⚑) by …" or "Nothing contested by …". `EvalCaseView.contestedRule` was added, and the README documents the setting.
- The backtest ledger (`backtest-ledger.ts`) records `rule?` and arms as `string[]`. RESULTS.md names the rule and joins arms with " vs ".
