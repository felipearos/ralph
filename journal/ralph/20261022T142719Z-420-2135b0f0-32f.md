## What changed
The dashboard reconciler now retries a loop that stopped itself with a doppler-scope failure: `failed` with lastEvent secrets-env, no-sigil or preflight-denied. It retries only when the supervisor is dead, at gaps of 5, 15, 45, 60, 60, 60 and 60 minutes. `RALPH_DASHBOARD_RETRY_SCHEDULE` overrides the gaps. Attempts are counted from `loop-retried` records after lastIterationEndAt, with an in-process retrySeen ceiling. When the schedule runs out, the reconciler sends one high-priority `loop-retry-capped` alert. push-notification gained both kinds and `--retry-*` flags, which become the raised line `retry`.
