## Key decisions

- **The loggd `TRIAGER_*` sections are not this repo's to write.**
  `fixtures/loggd/.ralph` is a mirror of loggd's `origin/main`, fenced by
  `fixture-mirror.test.ts`, so editing it here would break the fence. The text
  was drafted (`.ralph/logs/loggd-triager-sections-145.md`) and posted as a
  comment on #149, which owns the loggd-side rollout.
- `unblock` is described as unavailable (#150); no rescue bucket exists
  (#150/#155); no `<loop>-done` stamp on a triaged issue — in this repo that
  label means *merged*, and stamping it would eat a third of a routing batch's
  write cap. #131's "triaged by" stamp stays an unsettled gap, deliberately.
- **Two review rounds, eighteen real defects.** Round 2's value was catching the
  hole in round 1's own fix: a spoofable `plan.loop` would have let a plan
  rewrite and close another loop's in-flight ticket and then strip its `-wip`.
  That is the case `identityProblems` now closes, and it is the argument for
  re-reviewing a review's fixes rather than trusting them.
