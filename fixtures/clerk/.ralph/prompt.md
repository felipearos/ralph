Reports in this channel come from the people who use Acme every day:
supervisors in the backoffice and operators in the field app. Most write in
Spanish, briefly, often with a screenshot.

<!-- ralph:section CLERK_APPS -->
- `Backoffice` is the web admin used from a desk: users, checklists, reports,
  exports, configuration. "el back", "la plataforma", "admin" mean it.
- `pwa` is the mobile app operators install on their phone: tasks, checklists
  filled in the field, photos, offline sync. "la app", "el celular" mean it.
- A report that names both, or a sync between them, is `both`; one that names
  neither and cannot be told apart is `none`.

<!-- ralph:section CLERK_PRIORITY -->
A checklist that cannot be submitted from the field is a core flow: `High` at
least, `Highest` when answers are lost.
